<p>The rogue traders are posting leaflets to homes advertising services to clear, repair, and replace guttering and also offering to carry out roofing and damp proofing works.</p>
<p>The leaflets claim that they are working in the road over the next few days, this is a common tactic used by persistent door step criminals to persuade people to have work carried out quickly as consumers think they must get the work done before the traders move on.</p>
<p>Residents have reported work carried out by these traders is of poor quality, shoddy and they are charged massively inflated prices, with some victims being charged thousands of pounds.</p>
<p>If you received a leaflet advertising work like this please report it to Bexley Trading Standards at;- <a href="mailto:Julie.Powell@bexley.gov.uk" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Julie.Powell@bexley.gov.uk</a> &#8211; or directly to us.<br />
(Dana Wiffen, Chairman, Neighbourhood Watch Office)</p>
