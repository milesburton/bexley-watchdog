<p>It seems in this day and age it doesn&#8217;t really matter how much due-diligence you carry-out with tradesmen, rogue builders somehow manage to pop-up like a weed.</p>
<p>Since moving to Bexley these people have slithered out of the woodwork to ply their dubious trade.</p>
<p>Let&#8217;s start with <a href="https://bexleywatchdog.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/09/fd.png">Frank Dunthy</a> of unregistered <em><a href="http://directory.myhammer.co.uk/profile/all-weather-roofing-erith">All-Weather Roofing</a></em> Erith (originally registered at 56 whitehall lane, Erith, Kent, DA8 2DN)</p>
<p>When I arrived in Bexley my house was dragging along a garage which had a leaking asbestos roof. I thought I&#8217;d try out <a href="http://www.ratedpeople.com">RatedPeople</a> (26/11/2014) . In theory genuine feedback from previous buyers coupled with responsive tradesmen should be great &#8211; no, not at all.</p>
<p>Frank was one of the first people who responded to my advert on RatedPeople.</p>

<p>I Inviting Frank to my home, he seemed a fair tradesmen looking to build his profile. He had a few references from the local area and provided a reasonable quote &#8211; I was eager to go ahead and get shot of my leaking roof.</p>
<p>I decided to give Frank a quick call to reassure myself that his quote was genuine, and he would do a good job. Here&#8217;s his invoice:</p>

<p>One evening I decided to do a bit more research on All-Weather-Roofing &#8211; nothing, nada. This company didn&#8217;t exist. I decided to give Frank a call, he&#8217;d be reasonable so far &#8211; maybe he can explain. He once again assured me that he was straight up and would do a good job. To his merit, him and his team got to work within the week.</p>
<p>Let&#8217;s fast forward a while, Frank is making excellent progress and asked for payment to cover his material costs. Fair enough I thought. I&#8217;ll pay him the initial cut. I wasn&#8217;t too concerned as I&#8217;d offered him the opportunity to carry out further works worth three much more than this gig. Turns out this was a major fail on my part.</p>
<p>A few days past and his team were carrying out works on my neighbours properly <em>(ED: which I should add has been redone)</em>. Okay fair enough, I&#8217;ll wait a little longer &#8211; <strong>until&#8230;.</strong></p>

<p>Fantastic, water streaming through the area where they used a cheap flash band. Oh and that was against a rendered wall &#8211; good work. Great, the leak is now worse than it was before. Let&#8217;s give Frank a call.</p>
<p>&#8230;A few days later he arrives and come to &#8220;fix&#8221; his mistake. Rather than using lead or setting the roof into the rendering he threw some concrete on it. Fantastic, let&#8217;s have a look at his handy-work</p>

<p>Yes, that&#8217;s a light fixture hanging by it&#8217;s cord surrounded by asbestos dust. It&#8217;s a damn shame I can&#8217;t find the pictures of all the asbestos he left. At this point Frank disappeared.</p>
<p>I spend a day and a half, by hand clearing and washing down all the Asbestos.</p>
<p>It was only when I left him &#8220;bad feedback&#8221; did he decided give me a me. I received multiple calls how I was &#8220;ruining his business&#8221;, I doubt he needs my help.</p>
<p>He was reported to Environmental Health, Bexley Council &amp; Trading Standards.</p>
