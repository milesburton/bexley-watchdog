<p>Following on some excellent work by my electrician, it was time to redecorate the hall, landing, stairs and bedrooms. Most competent tradesmen would square this away in a month – allowing for drying time. Sadly <a href="https://www.mybuilder.com/profile/view/a1_star_decorating_and_construction">A1-Star</a> took 9 months and finally stopped responding to my messages – but not before they took almost £5,500.</p>
<p>If you&#8217;ve read my blog you&#8217;re probably asking yourself, seriously? Again? you&#8217;re either the world most unlucky, or stupidest person going. &#8211; maybe&#8230; Let&#8217;s see.</p>
<h1>Decor<em>struction</em></h1>
<h3>The Year of Optimism &#8211; 2016</h3>
<p>Way back when I had a simple idea; I&#8217;d like the hall &amp; landing to be re-plastered and decorated. My theory was, let&#8217;s start with the boxroom and see where we go. If those play out well, I&#8217;d like to continue on to the bedrooms.</p>
<p>Tradesmen after tradesmen. Dubious suggestions, or insane lead-times. After a while the now defunct A1 Star Decorating and Construction.</p>
<p>Tye Harber of Sladegreen and his builders mate Jay Eastwood arrived. They took a look at the boxroom, hall and landing.</p>
<p>I made the massive error of commenting on the quality of doors on their Facebook profile. Tye [Tyrone] Harber was eager to provide a quote &#8211; and I was ignorant enough to accept it.</p>
<h3>Eclipsed by A1-Star</h3>
<h4><em>Shut the door when you leave</em></h4>
<p>Jay &amp; Tye decided it would a be good idea to start by fitting the doors first &#8211; before the plasterwork. As it turns out this is a <em><strong>terrible</strong></em> idea &#8211; untreated wood draws in plaster dust, and any paint work &#8211; but who cares to a cowboy.</p>
<p>Oddly enough the door hanging went fairly well &#8211; doors looked pretty good. Sadly as any decent tradesmen would do, they didn&#8217;t bother to dot the T&#8217;s or cross the I&#8217;s. The keeps and frames were rushed &#8211; damaging the frames and generally leaving a piss poor job.</p>
<p><strong><em>Call me Mr Wickes </em></strong></p>
<p>Let&#8217;s fast forward. A1-Star started with the box room. A very common 3x4m space usually reserved for your dusty treadmill, or the first born.</p>
<p>It probably wont surprise you to learn these idiots didn&#8217;t bother turning up with the right tools &#8211; or in-fact <em>any tools</em> &#8211; except themselves. After listening to this moron curse for a few hours I suggested he borrow my steamer <em>(error)</em> &#8211; no thanks or otherwise as he snapped it away from me.</p>
<p>Later that day he walked out. Leaving my steamer covered in detritus and a damaged pressure valve &#8211; fantastic. I&#8217;m absolutely furious.</p>
